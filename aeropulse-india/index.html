NAQI & Telemetry Glossary

Central Pollution Control Board Standards & Metrics
Official CPCB NAQI 0–500 Scale
Tier 1 · Good 0 – 50
Minimal impact. Air quality is considered pristine and satisfactory, atmospheric exposure poses virtually no health risk. Safe for all demographic groups.
Tier 2 · Satisfactory 51 – 100
Minor breathing discomfort may develop in sensitive individuals with severe respiratory vulnerabilities, chronic asthma, or lung hypersensitivity.
Tier 3 · Moderate 101 – 200
Breathing discomfort to people with lung disease such as asthma, and discomfort to children and older adults upon prolonged outdoor physical exertion.
Tier 4 · Poor 201 – 300
Breathing discomfort to most people on prolonged exposure. Significant cardiovascular distress in people with preexisting heart/lung ailments.
Tier 5 · Very Poor 301 – 400
Respiratory illness on prolonged exposure. Pronounced clinical distress in healthy populations; severe physiological impairment for vulnerable elderly and infants.
Tier 6 · Severe 401 – 500+
Emergency condition. Triggers respiratory illnesses even in healthy adults. Imposes severe physiological distress on persons with heart/lung disease. Indoor air purification mandatory.
